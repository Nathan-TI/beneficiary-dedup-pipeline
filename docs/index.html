Recommended actions

Derived from this dataset. Each action has a suggested responsible unit.

How to read this

Actions are listed in order of expected impact and are recalculated each time new registration data is processed.

Remove 738 duplicate registrations before the next distribution

15.4% of registrations relate to individuals already on the list. Removing them prevents assistance from being paid more than once. See the savings estimate below.

Responsible: Programme / cash-based transfersImpact: High
Update the registration form to prevent the three most frequent errors

Phone number in wrong format (191): Only accept 10-digit numbers starting with 09 or 07. Sex not written as F or M (139): Use a two-choice list (F / M) instead of free text. Household size not between 1 and 20 (95): Only accept numbers from 1 to 20.

Responsible: Information managementImpact: High
Provide follow-up support to 3 enumerators with results well above the team average

ENUM-017 (high error rate), ENUM-005 (high error rate), ENUM-023 (high duplicate rate). See the Performance tab for details.

Responsible: Field coordinationImpact: Medium
Prioritise verification in Woreda 06, Woreda 04, Woreda 08

These locations have the highest duplicate rates: Woreda 06 (Somali) 17.8%; Woreda 04 (Amhara) 16.4%; Woreda 08 (Somali) 16.4%.

Responsible: Area officesImpact: Medium
Increase national ID capture (currently 32.0% of registrations)

Every pair referred for review lacks a national ID on at least one record. Where both records include a national ID, the system can decide automatically, which reduces staff time.

Responsible: Information management / national ID teamImpact: Medium

How it works

The process follows the steps a registration team would take before a distribution list is finalised.

Intended for: technical readers and data teams

Process overview

CollectRegistration data is received from the field. In this report, realistic synthetic data with known duplicates is used.
Validate10 validation checks identify missing, invalid or implausible values.
StandardiseNames, phone numbers and dates are converted to a standard format. A phonetic key links spelling variants such as Mohammed and Mohamed.
ShortlistOnly records that share a key attribute (phone number, national ID, date of birth or a similar name) are compared, avoiding 99.9% of comparisons.
CompareEach pair is scored across eight fields, with specific rules for siblings and conflicting national IDs.
DecideClear matches are merged, uncertain cases are referred for review, and one record is retained per individual.

Which data formats can it accept?

Registration data can be provided in the formats most commonly exported by data collection tools and databases.

Required and recommended fields

Required: first name, father's name, sex, date of birth.
Recommended: grandfather's name, phone number, national ID, region, zone, woreda, kebele, household size, registration date, enumerator. Missing recommended fields reduce accuracy and increase manual review, but do not prevent processing.

Column names

Column names are recognised automatically, including typical exports from KoboToolbox, ODK and SurveyCTO. For example, "First Name", "DOB", "Phone Number", "Fayda ID" and "_submitted_by" are matched to the correct fields, and grouped names such as "beneficiary/first_name" are supported. A template file is available at templates/registration_template.csv.

How to run it on a file

python run_pipeline.py --input registrations.xlsx --sheet "Registrations"
If a required column cannot be found, the system stops and lists the missing fields, so that no incomplete data is processed silently.

Glossary

Duplicate registration
A registration for an individual who is already on the list.
Unique individuals
The number of distinct people once duplicates have been removed.
Manual review
A pair of records that are similar but not conclusive, and which a reviewer must confirm.
Similarity score
A measure of how alike two registrations are, from 0 (no similarity) to 1 (identical).
Merge threshold
The similarity score at or above which records are merged automatically.
Correct merges (precision)
The share of merged records that relate to the same individual.
Duplicates identified (recall)
The share of all actual duplicates that were found.
Critical / warning
Critical errors prevent a record from being used until corrected; warnings should be corrected but do not block use.

Frequently asked questions

Is this real data?
No. All records are synthetic, and no real individuals are represented.
Which data formats can it accept?
CSV and text files, TSV, Excel (.xlsx), JSON, JSON Lines and Parquet. Common column names from KoboToolbox, ODK and SurveyCTO exports are recognised automatically. Full details.
Which fields are required?
First name, father's name, sex and date of birth. Phone number, national ID and location are strongly recommended.
Can it be used with our data?
Yes. Accuracy figures require known answers, so they are shown for test data only.
Who sets the merge threshold?
The programme. The Performance tab shows the trade-off so that the decision can be agreed and documented.
How can I request a change?
Use the Feedback tab.
