Accuracy against ground truth

The data is synthetic, so every true duplicate is known and accuracy can be measured rather than guessed. Exact matching finds only 33% of duplicate pairs. Fuzzy matching with domain rules finds 93.5% automatically with 0 false merges, and 95.2% once the review queue is worked.

Method

How it works

The same steps a registration team follows before a distribution list goes out.

GenerateSynthetic registrations with planted duplicates, sibling look-alikes and typical field errors.
Check quality10 rules for missing, invalid and implausible values, logged one row per issue.
StandardiseClean names, a phonetic key for spelling variants, phone numbers in one format, parsed dates.
BlockOnly compare records that share a key (name key, phone, ID, DOB), so far fewer pairs need scoring.
ScoreWeighted similarity across 8 fields, plus rules (siblings, different IDs, name-only matches).
Merge & modelAuto-merge confident pairs, queue borderline ones, build golden records, and model the results in dbt.

Glossary

Duplicate
A registration for someone who is already on the list.
Golden record
The single, most complete record kept for each real person after merging duplicates.
Match score
0–1 similarity between two records across names, date of birth, phone, ID, sex and location.
Review queue
Borderline pairs a person should confirm before merging.
Precision
Of the pairs merged, the share that really are the same person. 100% means no one was wrongly merged.
Recall
Of all real duplicates, the share that were found.
Blocking
Only comparing records that share a key (such as phone or name sound), so millions of comparisons are skipped.
Data-quality rule
A check such as "phone number has the right format". Critical rules block use of the record; warnings need a fix.

FAQ

Is this real data?
No. Every record is synthetic. No real person is represented.
Can it run on our data?
Yes: python run_pipeline.py --input your_file.csv. Accuracy scores need known answers, so they only appear for synthetic data.
Who decides the thresholds?
The programme. The explorer shows the trade-off, so the choice can be agreed and documented.
Something is missing or wrong?
Send feedback, and it becomes a tracked request.
